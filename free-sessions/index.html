Inside the pack

Three common tactical problems, fixed in training

Session 01 Play Through the Press A training exercise to improve positioning, passing angles, support, and finding the free player when building from the back.
Session 02 Press as a Unit Teach pressing triggers, cover shadows, supporting distances, and how the next player supports the first presser.
Session 03 What Happens When the Press Is Beaten? Help players recognize when to recover, protect central areas, and reorganize into a compact defensive block.
