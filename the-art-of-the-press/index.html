Your guide

A press built on one simple equation

We know how frustrating it is to watch a press fall apart. So we took the +1 / −1 principle used by Marcelo Bielsa's teams and turned it into something any coach can teach: one equation, twelve drills and six sessions.

Keep one more defender than their forwards. Press with one fewer than their first build-up line. Mark everyone else man-for-man. It works against any formation.
