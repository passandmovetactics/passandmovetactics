What you'll fix

Three match problems. One session for each.

Most coaches don't need more drills. They need the right session for the problem their team is having right now. Here are the three that decide how your team plays against, and with, the press.

Session 01 The problem: your team panics when it's pressed.

Play Through the Press

A training exercise to improve positioning, passing angles, support, and finding the free player when building from the back.

Session 02 The problem: your press is players chasing, not a unit.

Press as a Unit

Teach pressing triggers, cover shadows, supporting distances, and how the next player supports the first presser.

Session 03 The problem: one beaten press and you're exposed.

What Happens When the Press Is Beaten?

Help players recognize when to recover, protect central areas, and reorganize into a compact defensive block.
